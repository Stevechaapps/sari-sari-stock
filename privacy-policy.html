Privacy Policy

Sari Sari Stock · app package com.sarisaristock.app

Sari Sari Stock ("the app") is an inventory and replenishment tool for small retail stores. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information the app does and does not collect, and how it is handled.

Short version

  • All store and inventory data stays on your device. There is no cloud sync and no server copy.
  • No account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no location tracking, and no address collection are used by the app.
  • Payments and subscription billing are handled by Google Play, not by the app.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all app data from your device. There is no remote data to delete.

Data stored on your device

The app stores information about your store, products, suppliers, inventory movements, sales, shopping trips, and purchase history. This information is stored in a local database on your phone and is never sent to any server.

Voice input

The app can accept commands by voice. Audio is processed by the speech-to-text service provided by your device (for example, Google's speech recognition on Android) to convert your speech into text. The app does not record, store, or share audio recordings. To learn how the speech recognizer on your device handles audio, see your device manufacturer's privacy policy.

Payments and subscriptions

The app offers optional premium features through a subscription purchased using Google Play Billing ("in-app purchase"). Payment method, transaction, and refund handling is performed entirely by Google. The app only reads the purchase status that Google Play provides to decide whether premium features are unlocked. Subscription management and cancellation are available inside Google Play. Please see Google's privacy policy for how Google handles payments.

What the app does not collect

  • Personal or sensitive user data
  • Contact lists, messages, or files
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers or ad tracking
  • Crash reports or analytics telemetry
  • Email addresses or account credentials

Data safety

All data processing happens locally on your device. Data is not transmitted, shared, or sold to any third party. The app may use the device's internet connection only in connection with Google Play Billing operations and, when you choose to use it, the device's speech recognition service.

Data deletion

You can delete all data the app has created at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's data in your device settings. Because the app does not maintain accounts or store data off-device, there is no separate account-deletion procedure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available at this page.
